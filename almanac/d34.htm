Details from Stirling Albion Website:

Sunday 23 Jan 2000:
Whiteford looking to re-establish Saints Career

Central defender Andy Whiteford who is on a two month loan deal with the Binos from St Johnstone has spoken of his determination to win a new contract at McDiarmid Park. Andy's loan deal comes to an end with the Binos at the end of January and he is hoping to get back into St Johnstone's first team.

Speaking in a Sunday paper he said " Both Sandy Clark ( Saints manager ) and I agree a loan deal was the best option for me to keep getting games and stay sharp for when my chance comes to re-establish myself at St Johnstone. Even though I'm playing in a lower division I feel I'm learning a lot.

" Its frustrating to find yourself on the outside of the first team squad after getting a lot of appearances in the past. But I've just got to get my head down, work hard for Albion and hopefully that will earn me a chance I want"

Andy's deal with St Johnstone ends in the summer and he's eager to get back with them so he can experience big games again. He added " Walking into an atmosphere like Celtic Park was incredible. You could not hear a thing. An experience like that gives you a taste for it. I want to be involved in matches like that again and I'm determined to earn a new deal at Saints."

This will come as bad news to Stirling Boss John Philliben who must be thinking about extending Whiteford's loan deal until the end of the season. This Editor feels that would benefit both parties. There is no doubt Andy has steadied a shaky Stirling defence but an extension of the loan deal would give him more match sharpness and confidence.
